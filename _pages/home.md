<h3>Hey, I'm Raj!</h3>

<div class="home-main" markdown="1">
<div class="home-copy" markdown="1">

I am a Senior Research Scientist at [Meta](https://www.meta.com/) working on AI safety, investigations, and integrity. My work involves detecting, understanding, and disrupting the malicious use of AI across high-risk harm areas including scams, fraud, cyber attacks, and CBRNE-related threats. I conduct deep-dive investigations into sophisticated threat actors leveraging generative AI, automation, and evolving attack infrastructure. I also work on technical AI safety and alignment, improving model robustness, misuse resistance, and reliable behavior under adversarial conditions. Prior to Meta, I was an Applied Scientist at [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/) working on click fraud detection and fraud prevention for Microsoft Ads.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from [UC Davis](https://www.ucdavis.edu/), advised by [Zubair Shafiq](https://web.cs.ucdavis.edu/~zubair/). Before that, I completed my master's degree at [UC Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/), where I was fortunate to work with [Dawn Song](https://dawnsong.io) and [Hany Farid](https://farid.berkeley.edu).

Broadly, my interests span GenAI safety, online scams and fraud, and AI-based deception. I am especially interested in technical approaches for aligning AI systems with safety policies, evaluating adversarial misuse, and building scalable defenses against harmful model behavior. I have also previously written for [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/) and [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/).
